Random Rants

5.19.26

I wanted to start a journal for when I’m bored or just have some down time at work. I also like typing on my keyboard so this gives me an excuse to type instead of just reading documents. Anyways. Today is our second day. I’ve been enjoying myself. My experience is already so much better than the last time I worked here two summers ago. There are a lot more interns and the intern room where all our desks are has been renovated and looks super nice. I have a nice desk with two monitors and some drawers and cabinets. Pretty snazzy. Our first day (referring to my brother and I) went really smoothly. We got set up on everything and got our badges. Today we are just familiarizing ourselves more with what we’ll be working on. Basically, the gist of what we’re doing is working to help automate different software testing. I am excited to see what all it will entail.

Overall, the environment is great. Very laid back and pretty informal, which I appreciate because it takes off some of the pressure and anxiety that comes with starting a new job. Yesterday we went to the gym after work which I actually enjoyed. Usually I am just so tired after a long day of school or work that I just want to go home and nap, but I was actually feeling pretty happy and energized yesterday. It helps that my brother is here and his girlfriend will be staying with us for a little while. I hope to get back into running and other triathlon stuff later this week or next week. I want to get acclimated first and check out any routes and trails. There’s a Parkrun close so hopefully I’ll do that most Saturdays. I want to do morning workouts but I’m not going to push myself if I find that I’m really tired. Just going with the flow.

Right now, we’re pretty settled in. Everything is unpacked in our short-term apartment we’re staying at in Atlanta and it’s in a great location. Very convenient for errands and the drive to work is less than 15 minutes even with Atlanta traffic. No complaints. All I need to focus on now is getting into a good routine and making sure I don’t slack on my one online class I’m taking. But overall, the summer is off to a great start.
